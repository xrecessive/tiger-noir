# Traveling Tiger Center: A Case of Rain

A top-down noir detective action RPG set at the Traveling Tiger Center in Sierra Blanca, TX.
Four mysteries, a cast from the other Tiger games, manifestations of the detective's past,
and one real killer waiting at 04:00 with no bullets left.

Play it: https://xrecessive.github.io/tiger-noir/

Controls: WASD move, Shift run, left click strike / hold for charged spin, F flashlight,
RMB revolver, Q read, B breathe, E talk, Tab casebook, Esc/X close text, M mute, F11 fullscreen.
